# Intent

## Purpose
ApexDay is a free, design-first web app for people whose schedules keep changing (students first). It helps them plan a day of timed tasks, complete them, and stay motivated through visible progress.

## Goals
- G1: Users can quickly plan a day and complete its tasks.
- G2: A change in the schedule (holiday, day-order change, overrun) is absorbed in one reversible step, with nothing lost.
- G3: Users keep coming back, through motivation, progress by day, week, month and year, and fair streaks that never punish.
- G4: Users start tasks they would otherwise avoid, and partial progress counts.
- G5: The product looks and feels premium, is accessible, and works offline.
- G6: Users always stay in control and own their data: preview and undo, free of charge, exportable.

## Not goals
- No native iOS or Android apps.
- No social feed, public leaderboards or comparison with strangers.
- No team or enterprise task management.
- No AI that moves, deletes or reschedules without preview and confirmation.
- No selling or sharing of task data; no paid tier or feature locks.

## Success
- Retention reaches 45% / 25% / 15% at Day 1 / 7 / 30 (reviewed 90 days after public release).
- At least 60% of active users plan tomorrow before it starts, in a median of 2 minutes or less.
- No task is lost or duplicated by a shift, and at least 90% of streaks survive a first holiday or shift.
- At least 70% of planned tasks get some focus time.
