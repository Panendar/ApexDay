# Visitor can see a landing page with a live demo

## Story
As a user, I can visit a fast, SEO-ready page with an animated demo and a sign-up button.

## Purpose
People must understand the free product before signing up.

Serves intent goal: G5

## Behaviour and acceptance criteria
- The page loads with Largest Contentful Paint under 2.0 s on a mid-range laptop
- It has a live animated demo and a sign-up button
- It states that ApexDay is free; it shows no pricing tiers

## Order
Order: 65 of 70
Group: 6. Launch
Depends on: User can use a base theme with consistent design tokens
