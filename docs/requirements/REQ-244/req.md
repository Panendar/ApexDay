# User can opt in to anonymous analytics

## Story
As a user, I can choose whether anonymous product analytics are collected.

## Purpose
Success metrics need opt-in, anonymous data.

Serves intent goal: G6

## Behaviour and acceptance criteria
- Analytics are off until the user opts in
- No task titles or notes are sent
- The choice can be changed in Settings
