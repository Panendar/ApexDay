# User can choose from 8 themes and a density setting

## Story
As a user, I can pick a theme and Comfortable or Compact spacing.

## Purpose
Premium look and personal control.

Serves intent goal: G5

## Behaviour and acceptance criteria
- At least 8 themes: Light, Dark/AMOLED, Sunrise, Forest, Midnight, Sakura, Ocean, Mono; Auto follows system or time of day
- Themes change colour, the Apex visual and card texture
- Contrast stays at least 4.5:1 in every theme
- Comfortable (default) and Compact density

## Order
Order: 54 of 70
Group: 4. Progress and motivation
Depends on: User can use a base theme with consistent design tokens
