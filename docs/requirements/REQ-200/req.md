# User can close the day with an End of Day card

## Story
As a user, I can see tasks done, time on task, Top 3 result, streak status and a highlight, handle unfinished tasks and add a reflection.

## Purpose
Closing the day seals history and leads into tomorrow's plan.

Serves intent goal: G3

## Behaviour and acceptance criteria
- The card appears at a set time or when the last task is done and shows done, time on task, Top 3 result, streak status and one highlight
- Unfinished tasks offer roll to tomorrow, reschedule or drop; nothing rolls over unseen
- The day is then sealed; past days are read-only except a logged edit history action
- The card opens Plan Tomorrow in one click

## Order
Order: 18 of 70
Group: 1. Foundation
Depends on: User can complete a task in one click with Undo and a short animation
