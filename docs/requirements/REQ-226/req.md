# User can do morning and evening check-ins with energy-aware suggestions

## Story
As a user, I can tell the app my energy and mood and get suggestions on which tasks to do now.

## Purpose
Planning around energy helps the day go well.

Serves intent goal: G1

## Behaviour and acceptance criteria
- A one-tap morning check-in (energy: low, okay, high) lets the planner suggest tasks to do now
- Optional evening reflection is stored with the day
- Mood versus completion appears in Progress as an insight, never as a score
- Suggestions never move anything without confirmation

## Order
Order: 47 of 70
Group: 4. Progress and motivation
Depends on: User can pin a Top 3 and a day intention
