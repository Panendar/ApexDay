# User can reset the rest of the day

## Story
As a user, I can tap Reset my day and get a previewed, undoable plan from the current time with keep, compress and move suggestions.

## Purpose
A bad morning should still end with a realistic plan.

Serves intent goal: G2

## Behaviour and acceptance criteria
- Reset my day shows remaining tasks with suggested keep, compress or move-to-tomorrow and previews the result
- It reuses the Shift engine and is fully undoable
- When all remaining tasks are fixed nothing moves and the dialog offers to unfix selected ones
