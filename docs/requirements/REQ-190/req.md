# User can pin a Top 3 and a day intention

## Story
As a user, I can pin up to three tasks as the day's Top 3 and write a one-line intention.

## Purpose
The "if I only do three things" list focuses the day.

Serves intent goal: G1

## Behaviour and acceptance criteria
- Up to three tasks can be pinned per day and show a distinct marker
- Planning suggests Top 3 candidates from priority, due goals and unfinished carry-overs
- An optional one-line intention shows at the top of Today
- A skipped Top 3 task opens its slot for a replacement suggestion; a moved one keeps its marker on the new date

## Order
Order: 7 of 70
Group: 1. Foundation
Depends on: User can add a timed task to the day timeline
