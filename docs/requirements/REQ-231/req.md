# User can earn XP, levels, achievements and motivation lines

## Story
As a user, I can earn XP and levels, unlock at least 40 achievements and see at least 200 motivation lines.

## Purpose
Rewards without gimmicks keep people returning.

Serves intent goal: G3

## Behaviour and acceptance criteria
- XP scales with priority and duration with a daily cap; Top 3 tasks earn a bonus; partial progress earns partial XP
- At least 40 achievements across the categories in 8.4 including First Starter, Five Sprints, Rescued Ten, Comeback Start
- At least 200 motivation lines picked by context with calm, energetic and tough love tones
- After a shift the app says for example "Plan reshuffled. 6 tasks moved, nothing lost."

## Order
Order: 52 of 70
Group: 4. Progress and motivation
Depends on: User can complete a task in one click with Undo and a short animation
