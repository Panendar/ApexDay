# User can keep a fair streak with freezes

## Story
As a user, I can see a streak that treats holidays and rest days as neutral and offers streak freezes.

## Purpose
Harsh streaks cause churn; fairness is a retention feature.

Serves intent goal: G3

## Behaviour and acceptance criteria
- A day counts when completion reaches a user-set threshold (default 70%)
- Holiday and rest days are neutral; freezes (default 2 per month) are offered but never spent without consent
- Overall, category and routine streaks exist
- A frozen day shows a distinct icon on calendar and heatmap; freezes cannot be bought with XP
- Welcome-back flow after days away has no guilt and offers a freeze

## Order
Order: 40 of 70
Group: 4. Progress and motivation
Depends on: User can see progress by day, week, month, year and lifetime; User can preview, apply and undo a shift as one batch
