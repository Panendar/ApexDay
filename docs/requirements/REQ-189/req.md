# User can capture a thought to the Inbox and promote it to a task

## Story
As a user, I can capture an unscheduled thought in one line and later drag it onto a time slot or promote it with Enter.

## Purpose
A brain dump that is always reachable keeps planning fast.

Serves intent goal: G1

## Behaviour and acceptance criteria
- Pressing I or a floating button captures a line to the Inbox in under 3 seconds
- The Inbox shows beside the timeline; dragging an item onto a slot or pressing Enter promotes it with smart defaults
- A promotion that conflicts shows the planner's conflict warning and the item stays in Inbox until saved
- Items older than 14 days appear in a gentle "still want these?" review

Group: 1. Foundation
Depends on: User can add a timed task to the day timeline
