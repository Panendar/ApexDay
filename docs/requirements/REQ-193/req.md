# User can copy yesterday or last week into a day

## Story
As a user, I can copy yesterday's or last week's plan onto a day in one click.

## Purpose
Copying keeps planning under 2 minutes.

Serves intent goal: G1

## Behaviour and acceptance criteria
- Copy yesterday and Copy last week are one click each
- A preview shows what will be added before confirming
- Copied tasks are new tasks for the new date and do not change the source day

## Order
Order: 10 of 70
Group: 1. Foundation
Depends on: User can add a timed task to the day timeline
