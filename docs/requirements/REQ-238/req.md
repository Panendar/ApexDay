# User can opt in to email digests and rely on notification limits

## Story
As a user, I can receive daily summary, weekly review and streak-at-risk emails, with a daily limit and grouping.

## Purpose
Digests are opt-in and calm.

Serves intent goal: G3

## Behaviour and acceptance criteria
- Emails are opt-in with one-click unsubscribe
- Notifications are rate-limited (default maximum 8 per day) and grouped when several fire together
- Quiet hours, per-category switches and lead time are settings

## Order
Order: 60 of 70
Group: 5. Accounts, sync and data
Depends on: User can receive in-tab alerts and web push
