# User can enjoy layered animations and a weekly recap

## Story
As a user, I can see micro, day and growth animations that are skippable and capped, plus an animated weekly recap.

## Purpose
The feeling of progress is the product.

Serves intent goal: G3

## Behaviour and acceptance criteria
- Micro (400-700 ms), day (25/50/75/100%) and growth layers exist as in Section 8.1
- Every celebration is skippable and capped at one major celebration per minute
- Reduced motion replaces movement with fades and colour changes; animation hits 60 fps; no animation blocks input
- The weekly review plays a short recap of best day, longest focus and streak growth

## Order
Order: 50 of 70
Group: 4. Progress and motivation
Depends on: User can complete a task in one click with Undo and a short animation
